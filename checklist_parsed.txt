Complete every checkbox before public release. Critical items marked in red.


How to use this checklist
Work through each section in order. Every ☐ must be ticked before moving to the next phase. Items marked [CRITICAL] are hard blockers — the extension cannot ship without them. All other items are required but can be completed in any order within their section. When every checkbox in every section is ticked, Sentinel is ready for public release.
